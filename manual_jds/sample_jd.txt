Product Manager — Growth & Analytics (B2B SaaS)
Acme Analytics | Bangalore, India | Full-Time

About Acme Analytics:
Acme Analytics is an early-stage B2B SaaS startup building a data intelligence platform that helps mid-market enterprises operationalize their data for commercial decision-making. We are Series A, 40-person team, and growing fast.

About the Role:
We are looking for a Product Manager to own our core analytics product from 0 to 1. You will work directly with the founding team to define the roadmap, ship product, and drive growth. This is a high-ownership role for someone who moves fast, writes excellent PRDs, and can bridge the gap between technical engineering teams and business stakeholders.

What You Will Do:
- Own the full product lifecycle for our analytics dashboard product — from discovery and user research through to launch and iteration.
- Write detailed Product Requirement Documents (PRDs) and user stories for engineering sprints.
- Partner with the data engineering team to define SQL-based data models and KPI frameworks.
- Conduct user interviews and synthesize qualitative insights to inform roadmap decisions.
- Define and monitor product KPIs using our internal BI tools (Metabase / Tableau).
- Identify and size new market opportunities by conducting competitive benchmarking and market research.
- Drive 0-to-1 feature development for our natural-language querying module powered by LLMs.
- Work cross-functionally with Sales, Customer Success, and Engineering to build a GTM motion for new enterprise segments.

What We Are Looking For:
- 1–3 years of experience in product management, strategy consulting, or a technical role.
- Strong SQL skills — you should be able to write queries to validate hypotheses yourself.
- Experience with BI tools such as Power BI, Tableau, or Metabase.
- Demonstrated ability to ship a product from 0 to 1, including scoping an MVP and running a pilot.
- Experience with LLMs, Generative AI, or RAG-based product development is a strong plus.
- Excellent written communication — your PRDs should be clear enough that engineering never has to guess.
- Consulting, analytics, or market research background is a strong plus.
- MBA from a top-tier institution preferred.
